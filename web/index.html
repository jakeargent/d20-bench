d20 Bench

Same score. Twelve times the mistakes.

Erol Can Akbaba · 2026

I tested 25 AI setups on 120 questions about a game's rulebook. Two scored almost the same, 99.1 and 98.9 out of 100. One invented a wrong rule in 2 answers, the other in 24. Pick by the score and you would never see that gap.

A study like this shows you which AI to trust with your work, how fast it answers, and where it goes wrong, before a customer finds out. A rulebook works like a policy manual: clear rules, plenty of tricky cases, and answers you can check. Swap in your refund policy or your onboarding guide and the test works the same way.

Studies

Study 1 is an exploratory comparison of 15 AI models run on one graphics card. Each setup answered every question twice, with and without the rulebook passage behind it. Look at how many answers were fully right, how long a typical answer took, and how many added a wrong rule. What the AI read mattered most: the best setup went from 16 of 120 fully right to 113 with the passage, almost three times faster. Only three setups gave a typical answer within 5 seconds and got more than half fully right, and all three were Gemma 4, a model family from Google.

The totals cover all 120 questions. I reviewed 18 of them in full and checked the rest. The explorer shows those 18 public cases and their 900 answers, so you can read what each AI wrote. Read the report.

Study 2 is in progress and has no results yet. Study 1 handed each AI the right passage; Study 2 finds it with BM25, a standard keyword search, and tests Gemma 4 on the same 120 questions.

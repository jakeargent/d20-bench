# Provenance

Study 1 covers 25 configurations, 50 combinations of configuration and passage condition, and 6,000 answer records, measured in October 2026.

## Public cases and answers

The 18 human-reviewed public cases are listed in `../../../data/visibility.json`: 12 were selected randomly by topic and six deliberately. The release includes their questions, rubrics, oracle passages and 900 answer records, with the request text sent to each model.

Aggregate results cover all 120 cases per configuration and passage condition. The other 102 cases contribute only to these totals. Builds, tests and packaging need only this repository; the full-120 aggregates cannot be recomputed from the 18 public cases.

## The SRD source

The wording comes from the English SRD 5.2.1. Physical page numbers refer to the full main English PDF. The public oracle passages are included. See the [attribution notice](../../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) for the authoritative source and license.

## Scores and judgments

The published scores, final claim awards and correctness judgments include human decisions. Grader annotations are stored separately and can differ from the final judgments.

For the 72 public Gemma 4 E4B answers (36 thinking off, 36 thinking on), final scores and fully correct and material-error results are available, but per-claim awards are unavailable. Annotations from two graders are available for the 36 thinking-on answers.

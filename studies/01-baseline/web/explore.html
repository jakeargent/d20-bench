Explore the data

Erol Can Akbaba · 2026

This page lets you check the study yourself and read what each AI wrote. A configuration is one model with one thinking setting. 25 configurations answered 120 questions, with and without oracle passages, the rules passages used to write each question. That makes 50 rows and 6,000 answer records.

In the table, start with fully correct, then material errors, the wrong or invented rules an answer adds, which many people call hallucinations. A high mean claim score, the average points earned for required claims, can sit next to many of them. Then compare the median time, the middle answer, with the p95 in the same row, because a fast median can hide a slow tail. Further down, pick a public case and compare the answers with and without the passage.

I am publishing 900 of the answers in full, covering 18 human-reviewed cases. The other 102 cases are private and count only in the totals.

Quality and response time

All 120 cases per row, grouped by total-parameter size class. Times are in seconds. They include retries and leave out model loading and grading.

Each row has 120 answers. A few answers couldn't be graded as clearly right or wrong; those are left out, so some counts are out of fewer than 120. Modes off and on mean thinking off and on; on (low) means thinking on at low effort. Models labelled low or native cannot turn thinking off: low ran at low effort, and native ran with thinking on. Token means describe the responses. Times include all recorded attempts. p25 and p75 are the 25th and 75th percentiles and p95 is the 95th, all by nearest rank. The p95 is the slower end of the distribution, where 1 request in 20 is slower. With an even count, the median averages the two middle values.

The slow end

A typical response time can hide a tail of slow ones. The report's figure shows the median and p95 time of each configuration with oracle passages, grouped by size class, on a logarithmic time axis.
